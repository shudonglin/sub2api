Verdict for your situation

✓

Stick with Supabase (Recommended)

You're already on it. Singapore region. Unlimited queries. Same DB for both apps.

  • Zero migration work — just point sub2apipay's DATABASE_URL to your existing Supabase
  • 5-10ms latency from your Singapore VM
  • No 100K ops/month cap on free tier
  • Payment orders need reliable, fast writes — local region matters
B

Switch to Prisma Postgres

Better if you were in US/EU and wanted tighter Prisma ORM integration

  • 100K ops/month limit is tight — a few hundred payment orders with audit logs could eat through it
  • 150-250ms latency from Singapore to US/EU is painful for payment callbacks
  • Would need to migrate existing sub2api data or run two separate databases
